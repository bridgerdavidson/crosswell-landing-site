# Landing Page Brief

Source of truth for the Crosswell Consulting landing page. The site itself gets built in the repo (path TODO in the project `CLAUDE.md`); this is the content and structure spec. The audience is sophisticated finance people at investment funds, so it has to read credible and specific, not generic startup hype.

## Company facts

- **Name:** Crosswell Consulting.
- **What it is:** a consulting and software firm that builds custom AI tools and agentic systems for investment funds.
- **Target market:** private equity, private credit, and family offices (roughly $25M to $500M+ AUM), typically lean teams.
- **Team:** three people. A business/strategy lead, an ex-fund financial analyst, and an AI software engineer.
- **Core offering:** custom AI tools and full agentic "operating systems" that automate fund workflows (deal screening, diligence, reporting, operations).
- **Differentiator:** we have worked inside a fund. We understand the workflows from the inside, not just the technology.
- **Stage / direction:** actively building a full AI operating layer for a live private credit fund, and turning that into a repeatable offering for other funds.

## Suggested page structure

1. **Hero:** one-line headline + subline + CTA ("Book a call").
2. **Problem statement.**
3. **What we do:** AI tools grid + the Fund Operating System as flagship.
4. **Why us / the analyst differentiator.**
5. **Value statement** (time + ROI, use the before/after).
6. **How we work** (3 steps).
7. **The team.**
8. **Final CTA.**

## Section content (pull from the offerings and positioning files)

- **Hero:** lead with the one-liner, "Crosswell Consulting builds custom AI tools, software, and automations for investment funds." CTA: book a call.
- **Problem:** see `02 Offerings & Positioning/Positioning & Messaging.md`. Funds run lean, expensive people buried in manual work, judgment wasted.
- **What we do:** the 5 tools as a feature grid + the Fund Operating System as the flagship. Full copy and values in `02 Offerings & Positioning/Offerings & Tools.md`.
- **Why us:** the ex-fund-analyst differentiator. We live in both finance and tech.
- **Value:** the before/after table (screening, diligence, memos, LP reporting) and the "pays for itself in the first month" line.
- **How we work:** find the pain, build and prove it (fixed scope, fixed price), expand.
- **The team:** the three roles. (See positioning file. Names are TODO in the project `CLAUDE.md`.)
- **Proof (optional supporting copy):** realistic, unnamed case-study language drawn from `01 Clients/Active Fund Client.md`. Never name the client.

## Tone & style notes

- Professional, finance-credible, confident. Not flashy or startup-hype.
- No em dashes in copy.
- Speak to a fund decision-maker (partner, principal, COO), not a small business owner.
- CTA throughout: book a call / start the conversation.
- Don't invent client names or fabricate metrics beyond what's in these files.

## Brand

Use the "Fern" palette and voice. Full spec in `05 Brand & Assets/Brand Guide.md`. Warm, earthy, editorial, deliberately not aggressive high-tech blue. Ivory canvas, fern green accents, warm-gray neutrals, charcoal for dark sections.
