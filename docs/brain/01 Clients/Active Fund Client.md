# Active Fund Client (Private Credit Fund)

Real, current work for an active investment fund client (a private credit / lending fund). This is the proof of capability and the raw material for case-study-style copy.

> **Do not name the client** on the site or in any public material. Describe it as "an active investment fund" or "a private credit fund we work with."

## The big picture

We are effectively building the fund's entire AI operating layer. A lean team (about 4 people doing the work of 6) was losing time to fragmented tools, manual data entry, and lost knowledge. We are replacing that with a connected system of AI agents and automations that run their core workflows.

The takeaway for any messaging: we don't sell one gadget. We embed and build the AI systems a fund needs to run leaner, faster, and smarter.

## What we have built or are building

- **Shared AI knowledge base for the whole team.** One central brain the team reads from that gets smarter over time, so new context is captured once and never re-researched. New hires inherit the team's full context on day one.
- **Live multi-agent layer on top of it.** Scheduled agents run twice daily, read the team's CRM, Slack, and project tools, and automatically write deal status, pipeline changes, and people updates into the shared brain, then sync it to the team. Plus an automated daily briefing for each person.
- **Per-person intelligence agents.** Each team member gets an agent running four standing jobs: a daily briefing, pipeline/deal tracking, meeting prep and follow-up, and commitment/accountability tracking.
- **AI onboarding platform.** A new hire runs an AI onboarding coach that detects their computer's OS, installs everything, and troubleshoots live. Then a 22-question adaptive intake configures a personal AI agent tuned to how that person works. Distributed through a private code repo so the team pulls updates automatically; sensitive data stays out of it.
- **Meeting-to-task pipeline.** Recordings auto-convert into structured notes with action items, owners, due dates, and decisions extracted and routed automatically. In one demo it read a 2-hour strategic meeting and produced 5 personalized per-person briefs in seconds.
- **Daily-priorities assistant.** Scans each person's email, messages, and tasks and produces their top priorities for the day plus auto-drafted email replies written in their own voice.
- **CRM management and automation.** We run and automate their investor CRM (about 184 active opportunities across 5 pipelines): building scheduling calendars with multiple meeting types and availability rules, and keeping pipeline data clean. (CRM = Customer Relationship Management.)
- **System consolidation.** Migrating their text-messaging onto one platform with the CRM so the team isn't juggling separate tools.
- **Automated direct mail.** Built a CRM trigger to webhook to mail-house integration that fires physical postcards automatically off CRM events, moving toward AI choosing the right message per contact.
- **CRM-to-dashboard data pipeline.** A custom API flow that pulls CRM data natively into KPI dashboards for finance and operations, eliminating double data entry. (KPI = Key Performance Indicator.)
- **Secure applicant intake form.** Replacing manual application collection with a secure online form (file uploads, multiple parties, sensitive fields) wired straight into the CRM.
- **Automated monthly fund report.** An agent that reads connected fund data and auto-generates the recurring investor/fund report.
- **Content repurposing.** A tool that turns one long webinar into roughly 20 short clips for every platform automatically.
