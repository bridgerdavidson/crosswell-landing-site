# Offerings & Tools

The source of truth for what Crosswell sells. Two layers: standalone tools that fix one job, and agentic systems (an AI "operating system" for the fund). On the site, the tools show as a feature grid and the agentic OS is the flagship.

## AI Tools (point solutions)

Each tool fixes one painful, visible workflow. Lead with the before/after value, those numbers are the proof.

1. **Deal Screening Engine.** Ingests inbound deals from email and CRM, scores them against the fund's criteria, and surfaces only the ones worth a partner's time.
   - Value: turns hundreds of monthly inbounds into a ranked short list, cutting screening time roughly 80%.
2. **Diligence Document Agent.** Point it at a data room or PDF stack and it extracts key terms, covenants, risks, and financials into a structured summary.
   - Value: a 3-day read becomes a 20-minute review.
3. **Memo & IC Deck Drafter.** Drafts first-pass investment memos and IC (Investment Committee) decks from the fund's data and template.
   - Value: saves an analyst 6 to 10 hours per deal.
4. **LP Reporting Automation.** Pulls portfolio numbers and auto-builds consistent, branded investor updates. (LP = Limited Partner, the fund's investors.)
   - Value: turns a quarterly week of formatting into an afternoon of review.
5. **Portfolio & Pipeline Dashboard.** One live view across deals and holdings, replacing scattered spreadsheets.
   - Value: real-time answers instead of a day of data-pulling.

## Agentic Systems (the flagship)

6. **The Fund Operating System.** A connected set of AI agents that runs core workflows end to end, not just one task. Example flow: a new deal hits the inbox, the screening agent scores it, the diligence agent reads the data room and flags risks, the memo agent drafts the write-up, and the team gets a decision-ready package without lifting a finger.
   - Value: collapses a multi-day, multi-person workflow into hours and frees the team to do the one thing only humans can, decide.
7. **Custom Agents & Integrations.** Bespoke agents wired into the fund's existing stack (CRM, email, data providers, accounting) to automate whatever workflow is eating the most time.

## The Value (before / after)

The headline argument: your analysts are expensive and their judgment is your edge. We stop them from wasting it on manual work.

| Workflow | Before | After |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Deal screening | hundreds of inbounds reviewed by hand | a ranked short list, ~80% less time |
| Diligence | 3 days reading a data room | a 20-minute structured review |
| Memos | 6 to 10 hours per deal writing | a drafted first pass in minutes |
| LP reporting | a week of quarterly formatting | an afternoon of review |

Bottom line: a system that gives a lean team back 10 to 20+ hours a week pays for itself in the first month and lets them chase more deals without hiring.

## The 3-step engagement model

1. **Find the pain.** Identify one painful, visible workflow.
2. **Build and prove it.** Fixed scope, fixed price, deliver a working tool.
3. **Expand.** Once they see the time saved, build out from there.

## Direction

Crosswell is building a repeatable engine for fund-focused software and AI: proven tools and automations that drop into how funds already operate. The goal is to become the go-to technology partner for funds that want to move faster without hiring an in-house tech team.
