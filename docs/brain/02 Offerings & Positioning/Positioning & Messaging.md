# Positioning & Messaging

The credibility layer. Crosswell talks to sophisticated finance people at investment funds, so everything reads specific and credible, never generic startup hype.

## Who we are (one line)

Crosswell Consulting builds custom AI tools, software, and automations for investment funds.

## Who we serve

Investment funds: private equity, private credit, and family offices, typically $25M to $500M+ in AUM (Assets Under Management), running lean with small teams.

Specifically the people inside them:
- **Partners & Principals** drowning in deal flow they can't screen fast enough.
- **Investment / Deal Analysts** spending nights reading PDFs and rebuilding the same models.
- **Fund COOs & Operations leads** stitching together spreadsheets, email, and reporting by hand. (COO = Chief Operating Officer.)
- **Investor Relations** burning days a quarter formatting LP updates. (LP = Limited Partner.)

Not generic small business. Everything should read like it was built for finance professionals.

## The problem we solve

Funds run lean. A handful of expensive, smart people are buried in manual work: screening hundreds of inbound deals, reading data rooms line by line, rebuilding models from scratch, and hand-assembling investor reports. The result is missed deals, slow diligence, and a team's real edge (their judgment) wasted on tasks a system should handle. We replace that busywork with AI tools and agentic systems that do the heavy lifting so the team can focus on decisions.

## What makes us different

We have worked inside a fund. One of our partners spent years as a fund analyst, so we understand deal flow, diligence, and LP reporting from the inside. We don't translate between finance and tech, we live in both. Most AI consultants can't say that.

## The team (3 people)

- **Business & Strategy (Mikey):** owns the client relationship, scopes the problem, ensures solutions actually help.
- **Ex-Fund Financial Analyst (Max):** has sat on the client's side of the table; speaks the fund's workflow. The differentiator.
- **AI Software Engineer (Bridger):** builds the custom tools, integrations, and automations.

## The value statement

Your analysts are expensive and their judgment is your edge. We stop them from wasting it on manual work. (Full before/after table and ROI in `Offerings & Tools.md`.)

## Direction we're heading

Crosswell is building a repeatable engine for fund-focused software and AI: proven tools and automations that drop into how funds already operate. The goal is to become the go-to technology partner for funds that want to move faster without hiring an in-house tech team.

## Tone & style (non-negotiable)

- Professional, finance-credible, confident. Not flashy or startup-hype.
- No em dashes in copy. Ever.
- Speak to a fund decision-maker (partner, principal, COO), not a small business owner.
- CTA throughout: book a call / start the conversation.
- Don't invent client names or fabricate metrics beyond what's captured in these files.
- Brand promise: we find what's holding your business back and build the AI that moves it forward.
