# Brand Guide

The Crosswell brand. Same colors and vibe across everything: warm, earthy, editorial, and trustworthy, deliberately not the aggressive high-tech blue look. The feel is closer to a calm, premium, editorial brand than a flashy AI startup.

## Brand positioning (foundation)

- **Personality:** modern and technical, trustworthy and professional, with a premium and bold edge.
- **Voice:** confident, clear, and jargon-free. We sound like experts who respect the client's time.
- **Promise:** we find what's holding your business back and build the AI that moves it forward.

## Brand colors (locked) : "Fern"

A warm, earthy, approachable palette. Fern green signals trust, growth, and steadiness; the ivory and warm-gray neutrals keep it human and comforting for clients who aren't deep in AI.

| Role | Color | Hex |
|------|-------|-----|
| Base / background | Ivory | `#F1EEE6` |
| Primary accent | Fern green | `#4E7A4E` |
| Accent (dark / wordmark) | Deep fern | `#3D633D` |
| Neutral | Warm gray | `#B8B2A7` |
| Dark / surface | Charcoal | `#3D3A34` |
| Text | Warm near-black | `#1A1915` |

**Usage:**
- Ivory (`#F1EEE6`) is the primary canvas.
- Fern green (`#4E7A4E`) for the logo mark, buttons, and key accents.
- Deep fern (`#3D633D`) for green text on light backgrounds where contrast matters.
- Warm gray (`#B8B2A7`) for borders and secondary/muted text.
- Charcoal (`#3D3A34`) and warm near-black (`#1A1915`) for body text and dark sections.

## Voice rules

- No em dashes. Ever.
- Finance-credible, confident, specific. No startup hype.
- Speak to a fund partner, principal, or COO, not a small business owner.
- Jargon gets expanded the first time it appears (for example AUM = Assets Under Management).
